005-production · Pack encoder QKV in the production Metal path

conditional

Production implementation measured, adoption pending: Q4_K and Q8_0 show median paired reductions of 1.378% and 2.398% against a fresh baseline, with all 650 quality outputs and 90 latency texts per type unchanged. Q4_K regresses in the 20–30-second stratum and aggregate p90; Q8_0 has a slower second round. Permanent weight copies increase memory. An independent latency repeat is required before adoption; no implementation is merged.

This is the switch-free production trial, separate from the historical switchable prototype on measure 005. Its full, artificial-prefix and stage observations remain separate. No latency-repeat result is included at this checkpoint.

      Conditions and limitations

      Time from before sending the first PCM16 chunk through receipt of the terminal result. File reading, model loading and warmup are excluded. Each measured input is warmed once per fresh worker.

      Median over rounds for each input, then linearly interpolated p50, p90 and p99 across the measured input medians. RTFx divides total audio duration by summed request time.

      Literal CER: Total Unicode code-point Levenshtein edits divided by total reference code points, including punctuation, symbols and whitespace.

      Repository CER: NFKC followed by removing Unicode punctuation, symbols, separators and whitespace; no case folding or alternate-spelling substitutions. Edits and reference lengths are summed before division.

      • The current corpus is FLEURS Japanese test, with inputs from 6.36 to 28.2 seconds.
      • The p99 of a small duration-stratified sample is not a precise estimate of production tail latency.
      • The repository CER is not the published metric accepting alternative spellings of words.
      • Fresh-process load and first-request times do not reset filesystem or system Metal caches.
      • The candidate contains the production runtime from 37b7107 plus check-only source at 71c4fe9. There is no prototype switch; all measured environments are empty and the frozen runner rejects unrecorded GGML_*/SPEECH_* overrides.
      • Fresh baseline and candidate have 74 matching compile-flag files and 74 matching link files after replacing only source/build directory prefixes. The fresh baseline and candidate explicitly pass -arch arm64 and -isysroot; the fixed anchor omits both. Equivalent effective defaults are not established despite matching LC_BUILD_VERSION.
      • Q4_K improves 22 of 30 per-input medians, but its 20-30 s stratum has a -0.5924% paired reduction, and aggregate p90 rises from 121.78 to 126.04 ms.
      • Q8_0 improves 25 of 30 per-input medians, but round 1 has a -2.4706% paired reduction; its short round 1 is also negative. Do not claim a gain in every round.
      • The permanent copies add exactly 40.5 MiB for standard Q4_K and 76.5 MiB for standard Q8_0. Measured whole-process peak changes include allocator and sampling differences and need not equal these byte counts.
      • Load time is a median over three fresh workers with warm filesystem caches; first-request time is recorded separately and is excluded from warmed latency.
      • The six short inputs are 2/4-second prefixes of three recordings without prefix labels. CER stays null; their exact text agreement is not an independent recognition-accuracy measurement.
      • Only M2 Ultra Metal performance is measured. Windows Vulkan and other Metal devices remain unverified.
      • No additional stage diagnosis is used. Existing stage measurements remain separate from public worker latency and their gains are not added.
      • No model, type, decoding or thread default changes. No new GGUF format or model data. No prototype switch remains in production.
      • The ordinary automatic thread setting is 16 on this host. These requests explicitly use four CPU decoder threads; no default thread change or preliminary tuning gain is counted.
      • The official encoder check uses F32 weights on the unchanged separate-QKV path; CPU reference checks use 16 threads. Quantized Q4_K/Q8_0 packing is separately checked against the previous graph with four threads.
      • The separately linked official joint.enc diagnostic checks isolated F32/F16 projections from official encoded.npy. It does not execute packed QKV or establish full KDA/TDT reference equivalence.
      • The public worker exposes text and public token/segment arrays with timestamps. Raw internal TDT IDs, frame indices and duration choices are not observed by the full quality comparison.
      • The original prototype has separate source, checks, timing and evidence. Its historical asymmetric-check failure and correction remain on the prototype report and are not relabeled by this trial.
      • The planned independent latency repeat is not included here. It must retain its own 30-input, three-round block and must not be pooled with the original full or six-prefix data.
