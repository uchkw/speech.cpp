007b: Tile content attention with a relative-score mask

Source prepared only. No build, inference, accuracy result or speed measurement has been performed.

This independent Haas-ja-v1.0 experiment uses existing ggml FA_EXT for tiled QK, online softmax and PV on Metal. The dense positional product remains. It is not fully fused relative-position attention and attention memory remains quadratic.

  • SPEECH_HAAS_REL_FA=1 enables 24 unbiased layers, eight heads of 128 and 1–384 encoder frames (30 seconds is 375). An unsupported condition raises an error.
  • The F16 dynamic mask preserves head/query-specific relative indices, with two explicit F32 K/V layout copies whose cost is included. It changes mask precision and scale rounding. FA also changes K/V/probability staging and reduction order.
  • Original, candidate OFF and candidate ON must pass symmetric raw-output and recognition comparisons. Official CPU F32 90 dB and Metal 25 dB thresholds remain unchanged.
  • 650-input quality comparisons and stable complete-request gains are required before adoption.

Baseline: 114c514. Unchanged ggml: e1a9ac12.
