How to read these results

Median over rounds for each input, then linearly interpolated p50, p90 and p99 across the measured input medians. RTFx divides total audio duration by summed request time.

Every verdict belongs to its recorded build, model, inputs and settings. No pending entry claims a result. Changes to precision are judged with both error measures; higher precision does not guarantee lower recognition error.
