Experiment 034 — Haas Core ML encoder probe, source status

This page describes a source-only checkpoint of an independent experiment for the Haas Mac campaign: converting speech.cpp's pinned Haas-ja-v1.0 official NeMo frontend, encoder and RNNTJoint.project_encoder (restored unchanged in F32 from the pinned BF16 archive, lengths and masking preserved) into Core ML ML Programs, checking them against the official CPU F32 dumps, and decoding a verified candidate projection through speech.cpp's unchanged unboosted CPU-4 TDT decoder. Nothing below has been executed: no environment is resolved, no model is converted, no prediction is made, no number is measured. The pins, thresholds, disk plan and the runnable recipe are in pins.json and README.md; the remaining acceptance gates and what changed in this revision are in evaluation.md.

What is not claimed
